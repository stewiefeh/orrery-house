Terms

Orrery House is a fictional workshop created as a design and engineering exercise. The instruments, people, places, prices, and history described on this site are invented. Any resemblance to a real workshop is the resonance of good traditions, not a claim.

Nothing on this site is offered for sale, no orders can be placed, and the forms send nothing. Text and drawings on this site are provided as-is for illustration. If you borrow a phrase, borrow it kindly and credit the house; if you borrow an idea, there is no better compliment.

The easter egg (press T) makes a small sound. It is off until you press, and it respects your system's reduced-motion setting along with every animation on the site.
