Glossary

Ten terms that carry most of the trade. Each one is shorter than the argument it settles.

Escapement
The mechanism that releases the train in even steps, turning stored wind into steady time.
Fusee
A spiral pulley that evens out the pull of the mainspring as it unwinds.
Great wheel
The first and largest wheel of the going train, driven by the barrel.
Lunarium
A mechanism showing the Moon's phase and position against the stars.
Orrery
A mechanical model of the solar system, named for the Earls of Orrery.
Precession
The slow cone traced by the Earth's axis over some 26,000 years.
Ring set
The concentric engraved bands that carry the planets of a model.
Tellurion
A model of the Earth-Moon system and its seasons.
Train
The geared chain that carries motion from barrel to dial.
Winding square
The squared socket that receives the winding key.
