What a winding feels like

There is no faster way to understand an orrery than to wind one. The key is left handed on purpose; you cannot hurry it, and by the time the fusee is full your hand has learned what the mechanism is doing.

"A new orrery is not finished when it is assembled. It is finished when it has run."
Edith Marlowe, master of the house
