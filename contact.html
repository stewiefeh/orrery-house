Orrery House, Coach Lane, Ashcombe Vale, Harbour of Wrenmouth. In the world of this site it arrives on the morning boat and is answered in ink.

House hours

Visits Thursday to Saturday afternoons. Letters answered Mondays and Thursdays. The bench runs most of the year and stops for the Wrenmouth Regatta, which the house has attended since 1908.

Press and research

Students and writers are welcome in the visit room like anyone else. The archive is read on the same chairs.

Orrery House

Makers of hand-built mechanical planetaria in Ashcombe Vale since 1908. Every model is wound, listened to, and signed before it leaves.
